# Research and first playable scope

Research date: 7 September 2026.

## Kintara

The [public homepage](https://kintara.com/) puts its game world and entry buttons first. Public [spectator mode](https://kintara.com/play?spectate=1) loaded a rendered isometric world with a moving character. Its wallet chooser includes MetaMask, Phantom, Jupiter, Solflare, Backpack, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, Trust, Brave, Glow and Exodus, as well as an email option. No user wallet was connected during research.

The homepage and [Solscan link](https://solscan.io/token/Tqj8yFmagrg7oorpQkVGYR52r96RFTamvWfth9bpump) identify $KINS on Solana, contract `Tqj8yFmagrg7oorpQkVGYR52r96RFTamvWfth9bpump`. A wallet brand does not determine the chain of the game's token.

[Account docs](https://kintara.com/#docs-account) describe signed-message identity, saved progress, names/servers, free progression until level 10, a subsequent 1,000-KINS holding requirement and a 24-hour hold before trading. Their no-email description differs from the live chooser. [Gameplay docs](https://kintara.com/#docs-loop) describe gathering, crafting, combat, skills and quests. [Market docs](https://kintara.com/#docs-market) describe offchain gold that players can sell to willing buyers for KINS; the seller receives 95% and treasury 5%. This is not an automatic emission of tokens for every action. [Friends docs](https://kintara.com/#docs-friends) mark parties and friend teleportation as not live.

[CoinGecko's historical table](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/kintara/historical_data?start=2026-07-01&end=2026-07-08) reports $17,370,527 market cap on July 5, 2026. That verifies a substantial historical valuation, not its exact intraday maximum or the cause of the price rise. Its [September 7 row](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/kintara/historical_data) reports $1,184,392. Price ATH multiplied by maximum supply is not verified historical circulating market cap. Site-displayed player counts were not independently audited.

Transferable lessons: show the world before explanatory copy, make entry short, give a useful first objective, save identity/progress, and distinguish game credits from a financial reward asset. Do not copy the medieval aesthetic, gambling wheel, holding gate or MMO breadth.

## Touch Grass

The [homepage](https://www.touchgrass.family/) uses expressive branding, a sky background, short centered entry copy and a dominant lime action. It then becomes a long scrolling explainer. The [app](https://www.touchgrass.family/app) is a separate compact map interface. [Docs](https://www.touchgrass.family/docs) distinguish demonstration behavior from live transfers, with server validation of claims; the [roadmap](https://www.touchgrass.family/roadmap) distinguishes available and future distribution/features. These observations came from public HTML, styles and documentation; browser screenshot inspection was unavailable for this follow-up.

The user's correction takes precedence: Noobius has a fullscreen title screen with minimal words. Briefing, equipment and reward details live inside the game and dialogs. Its art, typography, industrial setting and gameplay remain original.

## Agreed v1 requirements

1. Fullscreen, clean title screen featuring the original Noobius character and a clear Play action.
2. Wallet connection and a readable signature create a saved employee identity; a temporary practice option allows immediate play without a wallet.
3. One interactive 3D room with a small recognizable Noobius, movement, station selection and visible restored-system feedback.
4. Three repairs: conserve 100 cooling units across target ranges; repeat a four-pulse GPU sequence; patch four labeled services to matching ports.
5. Three attempts per station; finished or unresolved incidents produce a shift report. Completed repairs retain their earned credits.
6. 100 credits/100 XP per fully repaired shift; 100/150/200-credit equipment purchases affect cooling tolerance, a boot replay and a network hint on subsequent shifts.
7. Wallet progress and best-shift rankings persist in D1. Guest progress is never trusted for wallet rewards. Empty leaderboards are honest.
8. A field guide, story, employee badge, equipment locker, sound control, supplied cinematic and clear token status complete the visitor experience.
9. No token launch, stock redemption, financial transactions, invented contract address or implied official Nebius/Robinhood affiliation.

## Stages followed

Research → bounded visual preview → game rules with tests → playable 3D room and puzzles → wallet/session/persistence integration → duplicate and cross-account protection → browser completion and responsive checks → private hosted delivery.

Future additional rooms or rewards require a separate expansion. This version already has a complete start-to-finish shift and progression loop.
